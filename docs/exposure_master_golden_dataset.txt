=========================================================
CAPITAL MANAGEMENT COPILOT
DATA DICTIONARY
Exposure Master Golden Dataset
=========================================================

Purpose
-------

The Exposure Master is the central dataset of the Capital
Management Copilot project.

It represents all balance sheet exposures and funding
sources of a simplified universal bank.

The dataset supports:

- Credit Risk
- Market Risk
- Counterparty Risk
- CVA
- RWA calculations
- NII projections
- FTP allocation
- Liquidity metrics (LCR / NSFR)
- IRRBB analysis
- Capital Planning
- SVA analysis


=========================================================
IDENTIFICATION COLUMNS
=========================================================

exposure_id

Unique identifier of the exposure.

Examples:
A001
A002
L001

A = Asset exposure
L = Liability exposure


legal_entity

Booking legal entity.

Examples:
DB_AG
DB_ITALY
DB_LUXEMBOURG


portfolio

High-level portfolio grouping.

Examples:
Retail
Corporate
Treasury
Markets
Funding


business_unit

Business line owning the exposure.

Examples:
Retail Banking
Corporate Banking
Treasury
Markets


booking_desk

Desk or function managing the position.

Examples:
Retail Loans
Corp Lending
ALM
Rates
Liquidity
Funding

=========================================================
CREDIT RISK PARAMETERS
=========================================================

credit_rating

Internal or external rating.

Examples:

AAA
AA
A
BBB
BB


pd

Probability of Default.

Example:

0.01 = 1%


lgd

Loss Given Default.

Example:

0.45 = 45%


Expected Loss Formula

EL = PD × LGD × EAD

where

EAD ≈ carrying_value
